Pick your expansion model

B2B uses Quarterly Business Reviews. B2C uses lifecycle cadence. Some businesses do both.
B2B QBR — What runs each quarter
  • Senior team, face-to-face, with the decision maker
  • Map each unmet need that surfaced since last review
  • Present the natural next service — not pitch, present
  • Decision-maker access matters more than offer design
B2C Lifecycle — What runs each cycle
  • Triggered by usage, milestones, or season
  • Email plus light personal touch from a senior team member
  • The next service is contextual to where they are in the lifecycle
  • Frequency beats depth — small touchpoints compound
